## Why "Canon"?

**Canon** originates from the Ancient Greek **κανών (*kanōn*)**, literally a builder's straightedge or measuring rod used to detect structural drift and ensure masonry remained plumb and true.

In Hellenistic philosophy, Epicurus adopted the term for his epistemology—**Kanonikē**—defining the *kanōn* as the objective criterion of truth used to separate verified reality from mental hallucination and conjecture.

In this project, **Canon** serves the exact same role for agentic software engineering:

* **The Straightedge:** An unyielding baseline that enforces architectural invariants and prevents codebase drift.
* **The Criterion of Truth:** Rigorous, runtime-grounded verification that filters out false positives and speculative abstractions before diffs reach human reviewers.
* **The Standard:** Compounding repository memory that turns one-off review feedback into immutable project norms.
